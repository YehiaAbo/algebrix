        Manual

        1. Aim, then act

        You never edit characters. Aim at a piece with the arrows (or Space and the letter over it), then press a key: m moves it across the equals sign the right way, s simplifies, d distributes.

        2. Both sides at once

        = - . / add, subtract, multiply or divide both sides. Type what, watch the live preview, press Enter. No Shift needed.

        3. Nothing breaks

        Every move is checked by plugging in numbers. A move that would change the answers resists, and the reason shows in the bottom bar. Dividing by something that could be zero adds a condition to the rail.

        4. Simplify when you want

        Nothing tidies itself. A dashed box marks what could simplify; press s to take it, or leave it if you want that form.

        5. Hold e for algebra

        Factor, expand, combine fractions, clear denominators, split into cases, solve for a variable (i), substitute from another equation (1–9). Arrows browse, releasing e applies.

        6. Hold f for functions

        Put a function on both sides, or press i to undo the outer one. ← → pick a family (powers, roots, exp and log, trig), ↓ steps in, release f to apply.

        7. Many equations

        n adds one, 1–9 switch. Answers are checked against the equation you started with; extraneous ones are flagged.

        8. The graph

        One variable: both sides as curves, and the answers are where they cross. Two: the solution curve. Three: the solution surface in 3D. It never changes while you work. Drag, scroll, double-click to reset.
